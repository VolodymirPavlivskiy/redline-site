Reporting a bug

To help us reproduce the problem, please include:

  • App version: in Confluence, go to Settings → Apps → Manage apps and find Redline.
  • Your site: the address, for example your-company.atlassian.net.
  • Steps to reproduce: what you selected, which action you took and in what order.
  • Expected and actual result: what you expected to happen and what happened, including the exact text of any error message.
  • Screenshot or screen recording of the Redline panel or form.
  • Browser and language you use Confluence in.

Please don't send confidential page content. If the problem depends on specific text, a short made-up example that shows the same behaviour is enough.
